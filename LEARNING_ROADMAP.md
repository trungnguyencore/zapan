# ZaPan v2 — Learning Roadmap

## Purpose
This document defines the learner journey, not the software implementation roadmap.
Numeric thresholds below are product heuristics until validated; they are not presented as scientific facts.
A stage is complete only when its required skills are demonstrated by the product's real progress model.

## Current product availability — verified 2026-09-24
Active learner stages currently backed by verified ZaPan v2 content and canonical progress are Stage 1 Hiragana, Stage 2 Katakana, Stage 3 N5 core vocabulary and Stage 4 N5 core Kanji.
The current verified repository contains 1,124 cards total: 46 Hiragana, 46 Katakana, 923 N5 Vocabulary and 109 N5 Kanji.
Stage 5 Grammar and all later Reading/Listening, consolidation/exam, N4 and N3 stages remain roadmap specifications only. They are not active learning content until their own source/content and review flows are inspected, versioned and verified.

## Stage 0 — Setup and orientation
Objectives:
- understand ZaPan navigation and review model;
- choose a starting path;
- establish a sustainable daily session.
Product behavior:
- explain Learn vs Review vs Practice;
- never overload a new learner with every game on the first screen.

## Stage 1 — Hiragana foundation
Skills:
- visual recognition;
- romaji/reading recall;
- reverse recall where appropriate;
- writing familiarity;
- common confusable pairs.
Suggested flow:
- small new-card batches;
- immediate practice;
- scheduled review;
- writing after recognition stabilizes.
Exit should require repeated recall over time, not merely opening every card.
## Stage 2 — Katakana foundation
Skills mirror Hiragana with additional focus on visually confusable forms.
Practice includes real loanword exposure when verified content becomes available.
Exit requires stable recognition plus scheduled recall.

## Stage 3 — N5 core vocabulary
Organize by meaningful topics rather than one giant pool.
Each word may include:
- Japanese form;
- reading;
- Vietnamese/English meaning as available;
- pronunciation;
- examples later when source quality is verified.
Learning uses new-card limits plus SRS review.
Custom Practice does not replace scheduled Review.

## Stage 4 — N5 core Kanji
Skills:
- recognition;
- high-value readings;
- meaning;
- Hán Việt where useful;
- writing/stroke familiarity;
- vocabulary connection.
Mnemonic text is treated as a memory aid, not factual etymology unless sourced.
Stroke-order availability must fail gracefully offline.

## Stage 5 — N5 grammar
This stage is enabled only after a verified grammar content set exists.
Each point should provide pattern, meaning/use, examples, contrasts/common mistakes, and practice.
Grammar progress uses suitable item types while remaining compatible with session/activity tracking.

## Stage 6 — Integrated reading and listening
Goal: stop learning isolated cards only.
Activities include short graded reading, sentence comprehension, listening recognition, and vocabulary/kanji in context.
Content must be versioned and sourced/verified.
## Stage 7 — N5 consolidation
Focus:
- overdue review;
- weak cards;
- slow recall;
- mixed-mode sessions;
- writing where useful;
- cumulative practice.
The product shows readiness evidence by domain rather than a vague total score.

## Stage 8 — N5 exam practice
Enabled when appropriate JLPT-style practice content is available.
Separate learning progress from exam-attempt analytics.
Track timing and section performance without corrupting SRS mastery.

## Stage 9 — Transition to N4
N4 appears as active learning only when its content and review flows exist.
The transition preserves N5 scheduled review while adding N4 material gradually.

## Long-term path — N4 to N3
Repeat the same verified structure:
- vocabulary;
- kanji;
- grammar;
- reading;
- listening;
- integrated review;
- exam practice.
Do not expose empty levels merely for visual completeness.

## Daily session model
A normal session combines:
1. due review;
2. limited new material;
3. targeted weak/slow reinforcement;
4. optional practice/game;
5. a concise session summary.
The exact ratio is a product heuristic and may change from observed evidence.

## Mastery philosophy
"Seen" is not "learned".
"Correct once" is not "mastered".
Mastery is derived from repeated successful recall with time spacing and acceptable error history.
Concrete thresholds live in the versioned domain engine and tests.
